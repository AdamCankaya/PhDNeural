Evaluating Data Sources & AI Tools

📅 August 2, 2026 👤 Adam Cankaya

Today, I am evaluating data sources for the remaining three diseases required in Plan 1. Finding longitudinal multi-omic datasets for Rheumatoid Arthritis, Type 2 Diabetes, and Epigenetic Aging is an interesting challenge that requires careful review. In parallel, I am experimenting with various AI tools to accelerate my workflow. Specifically, I am testing the capabilities of Cursor, IntelliJ combined with JetBrains AI, and ChatGPT Codex Code to determine which best fits my coding needs.

Plan 1 Status Update

Our Phase 1 data collection is progressing well, with the BRCA dataset successfully locked to the open TCGA-BRCA cohort.
